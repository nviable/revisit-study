# Task A

In this section you will see forensic techniques described one at a time, each paired with a scenario. Your task is to decide whether that technique could be applied to produce useful evidence in that scenario.

Each screen shows the scenario at the top. Below it you will see the technique's title, along with either the keywords or a set of structured tags describing the technique. When there are structured tags, you can select any of them to see a short definition of what it means.

Below that, three further pieces of information can be opened if you want them: the paper's abstract, a plain-language summary, and the full paper. You can open any, all, or none of them, in whatever order you like. **Decide when you have enough to answer. You are not expected to open all of them.** Use them as much or as little as you find helpful.

When you are ready, you will be asked whether the technique applies, how confident you are, and what you relied on in deciding.

Some techniques will fit the scenario and some will not, so judge each one on its own. If what you have seen is not enough for you to decide, you can say so.

There are 8 of these to work through. There is no time limit. **Once you move on to the next technique, you will not be able to return to a previous one.**
